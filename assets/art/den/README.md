# The den room

1600 × 1200 px, transparent, covering the whole den.

Upload files named exactly like the **File name** column, plus the extension
(`.png` is best: it also works in Shelly's Discord pictures. `.webp` and animated `.gif` work on the website), e.g. `back.png`. Use lowercase.
The website picks them up a minute or two after you upload. Until then, and for
any spot left empty, the drawn version stays.

| | File name | What | Size (px) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ⬜ | `back` | Window and tunnel | 1600 × 1200 | Sits on the wallpaper, behind furniture |
| ⬜ | `frame` | Burrow frame | 1600 × 1200 | The arch around the den, on top of everything. Keep the middle see-through |
