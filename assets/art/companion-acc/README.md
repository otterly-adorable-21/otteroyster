# Companion accessories

One picture per accessory, 256 × 256 px (or 512 × 512), transparent. The game lines it up on every companion (head, eyes or neck) and sizes it to fit, so follow the guide picture in the `_templates` folder: hats, bows, crowns and flower crowns sit with the bottom edge on the head; the bandana's middle goes on the red dot; the specs' lenses go on the two red dots (`specs` is for companions seen from the side, `specs-front` for ones looking straight at you). Want one made just for a single companion? Add `<accessory>-<companion>` (e.g. `tophat-crab`), 512 × 512 drawn right on top of that companion's picture, and it is used for that companion only.

Upload files named exactly like the **File name** column, plus the extension
(`.png` is best: it also works in Shelly's Discord pictures. `.webp` and animated `.gif` work on the website), e.g. `bow.png`. Use lowercase.
The website picks them up a minute or two after you upload. Until then, and for
any spot left empty, the drawn version stays.
