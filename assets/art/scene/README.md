# The pond scene

The picture behind your otter on the Otter page, 1600 × 1000 px. The pond box is wide on computers and narrower on phones, so keep the important parts in the middle 1100 px; the left and right edges get trimmed on phones. Your otter stands in the bottom middle. Uploading a scene replaces the drawn willows, lily pads, lilies, sky and water shimmer. Layers, bottom to top: the background (still), `water` (your hand-animated WebP/APNG of the water), the lily pads `lily1`–`lily8` (one transparent PNG each on the full 1600 × 1000 canvas, the pad drawn at its size exactly where it floats; the game makes each drift gently at its own pace), then `overlay` (anything else animated, e.g. swaying reeds). All sit under the otter.

Upload files named exactly like the **File name** column, plus the extension
(`.png` is best: it also works in Shelly's Discord pictures. `.webp` and animated `.gif` work on the website), e.g. `base.png`. Use lowercase.
The website picks them up a minute or two after you upload. Until then, and for
any spot left empty, the drawn version stays.
